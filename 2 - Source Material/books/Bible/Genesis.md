Genesis 2:2-3  "By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done"
**God started by heavens and earth, then light, then he created the vault between the water which was the sky. After he created land which was able to produce vegetation. He then created the sun and the moon/stars to govern the day and night. After he created birds to fly and sea creatures for the water to increase in numbers and population. After he created land animals and then created humans in his image to populate and govern using the animals and vegetation given by God. The seventh day God rested from all his creation and made the day holy.

Genesis 2:4-6 " 'You will certainly not die,' the serpent said to the women. 'For God knows that when you eat from it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and evil.' When the woman saw that the fruit of the tree was good for food  and pleasing to the eye, and also desirable for gaining wisdom, she took some and ate it."

**Punishments for eating the fruit:

	**Serpent: He will forever crawl on its belly, put enmity between it and woman/child.
	**Woman: Making child bearing very severe and painful. Making desire their husband who will rule them.
	**Man: Cursed the ground with sin, and created hunger? Human was banished from Eden and no longer allowed to eat from the Tree of life which he would live forever.

Cain and Abel:
- Both bring food to the Lord, Abel's is taken and Cain's is not
- Cain gets mad at this and kills his brother Abel
- God curses Cain by never letting him yield crops again and he will be a restless wanderer
- Lord promises if anyone kills Cain will suffer vengeance.
The descendants of Cain became wicked and evil, very sinful. God granted Adam and Eve another son who they named Seth.

Noah: Descendant of Adam and Seth.
 - The world had become so filled with sin and evil, the LORD decided to wipe everything out. The LORD took likeness of Noah since he and his family were the only humans who still followed and praised the LORD.
 - 3 Sons: Shem, Ham (Ham was the father of Canaan), and Japheth
 - God commanded Noah to build an arc, as he was going to wipe the world out with floodwaters. Noah was commanded to take his family, food, and 2 of every animal (one male and one female).
 - Noah sacrifices clean animals to God as a thank you
 Genesis 8:21"Never again will i curse the ground because of humans, even though every inclination of the human heart is evil from childhood. And never again will I destroy all living creatures, as I have done."
 - God's Covenant with Noah: God will not destroy the world or mankind ever again, to remind the world of this, he will show rainbows in the sky in the clouds.
 - Noah got drunk, Ham mocked Noah and disrespected him. Because of this, Noah cursed Ham's son Canaan and made him a slave of Shem and Japheth
 - Clans of Noah's sons:
	 - The Japhethites
	 - The Hamites
	 - The Semites
 - The Semites were sons who were born to Shem.


The tower of Babel:
	People all spoke one language, after settling down they decided to build a tower to heaven. After great progress people started devoted their live to this tower and praising it more than God. As a punishment to stop this, God came down and changed their languages so they could not understand each other, he then scattered these people across the world.
